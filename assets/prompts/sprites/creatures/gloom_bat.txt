A pixel-art animation sprite sheet of one creature for a dark fantasy top-down role-playing game: a gloom bat from the old mine entrance, a big cave bat. Leathery wings of soot black and dark brown, a furry dark brown body, big pointed ears, small pale amber eyes, tiny white fangs. It is always in the air, a little above the ground.

Eight rows of frames, each row a separate strip, every frame the same bat at the same scale on the same ground line. The camera always looks down at it from about 45 degrees, so its top shows in every view:
- Row 1, four frames: the bat flying, flapping its wings, toward the camera, seen from above and in front (we see its face, ears and the fronts of its wings, with its back above them; the wings alternate).
- Row 2, four frames: the bat flying, flapping its wings, away from the camera, seen from above and behind (we see its back and the backs of its wings; the wings alternate).
- Row 3, four frames: the bat flying, flapping its wings, to the right, in profile facing right.
- Row 4, six frames: the bat hovering in place, beating its wings slowly: two frames facing the camera, two frames seen from behind, two frames in profile facing right.
- Row 5, three frames: the bat biting toward the camera, seen from above and in front (draws back, darts in with its fangs bared, pulls away).
- Row 6, three frames: the bat biting away from the camera, seen from above and behind (draws back, darts in with its fangs bared, pulls away).
- Row 7, three frames: the bat biting to the right, in profile facing right (draws back, darts in with its fangs bared, pulls away).
- Row 8, three frames: the bat dying, seen from the front (falls out of the air, crumples, lies still on the ground with its wings spread).

The same silhouette, proportions, colours and size in every frame: only the pose and the facing change.

Hard technical requirements, these matter more than the art direction:
- Solid flat magenta background, exactly RGB(255, 0, 255), filling every pixel that is
  not part of a drawing. No gradient, no vignette, no texture, no shadow cast onto it.
- Lay the subjects out in an even grid with equal spacing. Every cell the same size.
  No labels, no captions, no text of any kind anywhere in the image.
- Every subject fully inside its own cell, centred, touching neither its neighbours nor
  the edge of the image. In an animation, the same size and the same ground line in
  every cell.
- Three-quarter top-down view: the camera looks down from the south at about 45
  degrees, so the top and the south face of things show. Light from the top left.
- Pixel art: crisp square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft
  airbrush shading. Limited palette, flat shading in three or four tones, and a dark
  outline of the local colour (never pure black).
- Dark fantasy colours: muted and earthy, with one brighter accent at most. Nothing in
  a subject may be magenta, pink, purple or violet: those hues are the background key.
- They are read at 32x32 pixels in game (people at 64x64), so favour a strong
  silhouette and clear separation of parts over fine detail.
