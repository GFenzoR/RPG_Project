A pixel-art animation sprite sheet of one creature for a dark fantasy top-down role-playing game: a tide crab of the harbour island shore, a crab as big as a dog. A heavy rough shell of dull slate blue-grey crusted with barnacles and specks of sea moss, one claw much bigger than the other, rusty orange-brown legs and claw tips, two black eyes on short stalks.

Eight rows of frames, each row a separate strip, every frame the same crab at the same scale on the same ground line. The camera always looks down at it from about 45 degrees, so its top shows in every view:
- Row 1, four frames: the crab scuttling toward the camera, seen from above and in front (we see its eye stalks, claws and the front of its shell, with its back above them; the legs alternate).
- Row 2, four frames: the crab scuttling away from the camera, seen from above and behind (we see its the back of its shell and its legs; the legs alternate).
- Row 3, four frames: the crab scuttling to the right, in profile facing right.
- Row 4, six frames: the crab standing still, claws twitching: two frames facing the camera, two frames seen from behind, two frames in profile facing right.
- Row 5, three frames: the crab pinching toward the camera, seen from above and in front (raises its big claw, snaps it shut, lowers it).
- Row 6, three frames: the crab pinching away from the camera, seen from above and behind (raises its big claw, snaps it shut, lowers it).
- Row 7, three frames: the crab pinching to the right, in profile facing right (raises its big claw, snaps it shut, lowers it).
- Row 8, three frames: the crab dying, seen from the front (flips onto its back, legs twitching, then lies still).

The same silhouette, proportions, colours and size in every frame: only the pose and the facing change.

Hard technical requirements, these matter more than the art direction:
- Solid flat magenta background, exactly RGB(255, 0, 255), filling every pixel that is
  not part of a drawing. No gradient, no vignette, no texture, no shadow cast onto it.
- Lay the subjects out in an even grid with equal spacing. Every cell the same size.
  No labels, no captions, no text of any kind anywhere in the image.
- Every subject fully inside its own cell, centred, touching neither its neighbours nor
  the edge of the image. In an animation, the same size and the same ground line in
  every cell.
- Three-quarter top-down view: the camera looks down from the south at about 45
  degrees, so the top and the south face of things show. Light from the top left.
- Pixel art: crisp square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft
  airbrush shading. Limited palette, flat shading in three or four tones, and a dark
  outline of the local colour (never pure black).
- Dark fantasy colours: muted and earthy, with one brighter accent at most. Nothing in
  a subject may be magenta, pink, purple or violet: those hues are the background key.
- They are read at 32x32 pixels in game (people at 64x64), so favour a strong
  silhouette and clear separation of parts over fine detail.
